**Aether Prism: Tide of Shadows** is a full-bleed atmospheric tactical roguelite.
You command an ancient Solar Observatory and its Prism Relay Network on a twilight
archipelago under siege by Void Leviathans and Shadow Fleets. Place and rotate
Optical Prisms, Gravity Wells and Tesla Mirrors across islands and bridges to bend,
split and amplify the Solar Lighthouse Beam into devastating geometric laser webs —
then fire kinetic pulse-torpedoes and trigger overloaded prism chain-detonations.
Survive all 7 nights and shatter the Void Leviathan.

## How to play

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Click a dock card, then a build pad | Place a Prism / Splitter / Mirror / Gravity Well |
| Click a placed prism | Rotate it 45° (beam bends with it) |
| Shift-click a placed prism | Sell it (50% refund) |
| 1–6 | Dock cards: Prism, Splitter, Mirror, Well, Torpedo, Chain Nova |
| Click with Torpedo armed | Fire a homing pulse-torpedo at the target |
| Click a prism with Chain Nova armed | Overload-detonate it (huge AoE, prism is destroyed) |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| R | Restart |

## The loop

- **Bend the light** — the Lighthouse Beam refracts through every prism you place.
  Splitters fork it into two beams; Tesla Mirrors bounce it 90°; Gravity Wells curve
  it toward them and slow nearby enemies. A beam that crosses an enemy burns it.
- **Hold the Core** — Shadow Fleets sail in from the dark sea toward the Observatory.
  Anything that reaches the Core rams it and drains Core Integrity.
- **Choose your route** — after every cleared night, the Expedition Map opens: pick
  a branching route node, then choose 1 of 3 boons (relics) for your network.
- **Win / lose** — kill the Void Leviathan on Night 7 for a Dawn Victory. If Core
  Integrity hits zero, the light goes out.
